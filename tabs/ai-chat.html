Privacy Policy & Data Usage
Your Privacy is My Priority: This application does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information or email data. Your data will not be used for training AI models according to Gemini's data governance policy for paid API.

What Data I Access

Gmail Cleaner AI requires access to your Gmail account to:

  • Read email metadata and content for categorization purposes
  • Identify and filter junk mail, promotional emails, and unwanted subscriptions
  • Delete or unsubscribe from identified junk emails upon your confirmation

How I Use Your Data

Your email data is used exclusively for the following purposes:

  • Email Categorization: AI analysis to intelligently categorize your emails into relevant groups
  • Junk Mail Detection: Identify spam, promotional content, and unwanted subscriptions
  • Automated Cleanup: Delete or unsubscribe from junk mail with your explicit permission

Data Retention & Security

Zero Data Storage: I do not store any of your email content or personal information on my servers.
  • Immediate Token Deletion: OAuth access tokens are deleted immediately after each task is completed
  • No Data Collection: I do not collect, log, or retain any email content, metadata, or personal information
  • Secure Processing: All email analysis is performed in real-time and data is discarded after processing
  • Private Infrastructure: This tool runs on private infrastructure and is not accessible from the public internet

Third-Party Services

This application uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Gmail API: To access and manage your emails (subject to Google's privacy policy)
  • Google Gemini AI (Paid Tier): For intelligent email categorization. I use the paid version of Gemini, which ensures your data is not used for model training or improvement purposes

Data Sharing

No Third-Party Sharing: Your email data is never shared with, sold to, or disclosed to any third parties.
  • Email data is only sent to Google Gemini AI API for analysis and is processed according to Google's enterprise data processing terms
  • No data is stored, logged, or transmitted to any other external services
  • No analytics, tracking, or monitoring tools are used on your email data

You maintain full control over your data:
