Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for visiting my portfolio. I respect your privacy and am committed to protecting it. This policy explains the limited ways in which this website interacts with your data.

2. Information Collection

This is a static portfolio website designed to showcase my professional work. I do not actively collect, store, or process personal data from visitors, with the exception of voluntarily provided communication.

3. Third-Party Services

This website integrates Tawk.to, a third-party live chat service, to allow real-time communication.

  • When you use the chat widget, Tawk.to may collect information such as your IP address, browser type, and operating system to facilitate the chat session.
  • If you voluntarily provide your name or email address during a chat, that information is processed by Tawk.to and made available to me solely for the purpose of replying to your inquiry.

Please refer to Tawk.to's Privacy Policy for more details on how they handle data.

4. Cookies

This website itself does not use cookies for tracking or advertising. However, the Tawk.to chat widget may set necessary cookies to maintain your chat session and preferences.

5. Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please feel free to contact me using the "message me" app on this site.
