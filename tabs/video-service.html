Advanced Filter Chain

We use a Filter Complex to process the video once. The despill filter removes green color cast from the subject edges, while split=2 enables simultaneous encoding for maximum efficiency.

ffmpeg -i input.mp4 \ -filter_complex \ "[0:v]colorkey=0x00FF00:0.15:0.1,despill=green,scale='min(720,iw)':'-2',split=2[android][ios]" \ -map "[android]" -c:v libvpx-vp9 out.webm \ -map "[ios]" -tag:v hvc1 out.mov
